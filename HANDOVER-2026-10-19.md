## inercia — what the refactor changed

One library (`inercia/src/inercia/`) now owns loading, signals, backtest,
statistics and artifacts. The old scripts are thin wrappers. `src/run.py` is the
recommended entry point.

Fixed:

- `sleeve.py` used `wd.mul(scale, axis=1)`; the scale Series aligned to ticker
  columns, producing NaN weights, zero turnover, and zero costs. Correct axis is 0.
- Full-sample `r.std()` volatility scaling in `book.py`/`combo.py` was a
  look-ahead; the canonical backtest uses rolling 60-day vol shifted one day.
- FRED monthly-average policy rates are now lagged two months before FX carry use.
- One excess-return convention; one performance function; one trial registry.

## Pitfalls (don't rediscover)

- Paths contain apostrophes (`l'Observatori`, `nit_d'alba`) — double-quote every
  shell occurrence.
- FRED: use the DEFAULT User-Agent; `fredgraph.csv?id=SERIES`, no key.
- Yahoo: raw HTTP 429s — use the `yfinance` client.
- CBOE index histories are free; VIX futures are 403.
- VIX3M starts 2009-09-18; SVXY changed leverage in Feb 2018.
- Local FRED cache ends at different dates per series (some 2026-01, some
  2026-08); rerun `fetch_carry.py` for fresh rates.
- Pandas 3: `resample("ME")` and `resample("YE")` are the current aliases.
- When multiplying a DataFrame by a date-indexed Series, use `axis=0`; `axis=1`
  aligns to columns and silently produces NaNs.
