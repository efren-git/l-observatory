# AUDIT — cartograf (2026-10-19)

_What was wrong, how it was found, and what changed. Reproduce with the scripts in
`diagnostics/`; the numbers below are the ones in `output/*.json` after the fixes._

Scope: the refactor that moved cartograf onto one shared package
(`src/cartograf/`), plus five defects found while reconciling the book with the
JSONs. The point of this file is to keep the failures visible, not just the final
numbers.

## 1. Costs were never charged (axis bug)

**Symptom.** A monthly-rebalanced book reported turnover exactly 0.0.

**Cause.** `wd.mul(scale, axis=1)` aligned a date-indexed scale Series to ticker
*columns*. The result was all-NaN weights, zero turnover, zero costs.

**Fix.** `axis=0`. Same convention as `inercia`: weights are gross-normalised,
costs are 5 bps per unit turnover (10 bps for the vol ETFs), and turnover is
reported instead of assumed away.

**Effect.** In `inercia` the stock XS-momentum sleeve fell from Sharpe +0.23 to
+0.121 once 10 bps were actually charged. In `cartograf` the equity momentum
sleeve is +0.106 net; its turnover is now 8.9×/yr, not zero. Any pre-fix Sharpe
that depended on that sleeve was overstated.

## 2. Volatility scaling used the full sample (look-ahead)

**Symptom.** Book drawdowns looked better than the sleeves could support.

**Cause.** `book.py`/`combo.py` scaled by `r.std()` over the whole sample, so
2008 knew the vol of 2026.

**Fix.** Rolling 60-day vol, shifted one day: the scale used on day *t* is
estimated only from data through *t−1*. Weights are scaled, not returns, so the
cost of the leverage is charged.

**Effect (before the live-name mask in §5).** Equal-weight 0.62 → 0.66; inverse-vol
0.52 → 0.55; HRP 0.37 → 0.39; core 0.72 → 0.72. The 2008 equal-weight book return fell
from +13.2% to +10.0%. The live-name mask then repriced the same books to 0.58 / 0.56 /
0.34 / 0.61; the "insurance" was flattered by both defects.

## 3. FX carry: current-month rates and a missing foreign rate

Two defects in the same leg:

1. **Publication timing.** FRED's policy-rate series are monthly averages. The
   old code used month *t*'s average from day one of month *t*. Monthly averages
   are now lagged **two months** before use.
2. **The wrong return.** The code subtracted the US rate from an FX ETF return
   but never added the foreign rate. The diagnostic shows FXE tracks spot to
   2.6 bp/month, i.e. the ETF is not accruing the foreign deposit rate. The leg
   was therefore a spot bet minus US cash, not a carry bet.

**Fix.** Carry signal = publication-lagged foreign rate − US rate. Leg return =
FX ETF return + lagged foreign rate − US rate.

**Effect.** FX carry Sharpe 0.01 → **0.32**. The carry book −0.13 → **0.08**.
Adding 30% of the book to a 60/40 still lowers Sharpe 0.774 → 0.735. The
conclusion (carry does not earn its place) survives; the stated *reason* had to
change, because "FX carry is flat" was a bug, not a fact.

## 4. VRP proxy warm-up regression (found during this reconciliation)

**Symptom.** The legacy SVXY proxy suddenly scored 0.41 at 24.8% vol, versus
0.10 at 9.2% vol in the previous book.

**Cause.** The refactor filled the missing vol-target scale with `1.0`
(`fillna(1.0)`). That held **raw** SVXY — realised vol ~52% — for the first 24
months, and those months dominated the series. It was not a cost effect and not
a data effect; it was a warm-up convention.

**Fix.** `fillna(0.0)`: do not hold the position until the trailing vol estimate
exists. C returns to 0.005 CAGR / 0.091 vol / **0.102 Sharpe**, matching the
pre-refactor row and restoring the like-for-like comparison with B (0.898).

**Lesson.** For a vol-targeted series, a warm-up fill of 1.0 is never neutral;
it is full leverage at unknown vol. Fill with 0 and lose the months, or start the
sample later — never silently invest.

## 5. Phantom cross-sections (live-name mask)

**Symptom.** BAB's 2008–2010 score was 95–97% composed of ETFs that had not listed yet.

**Cause.** R = px.pct_change().fillna(0.0) turns a missing price into a zero
return. A pre-inception fund therefore had beta = 0 and vol = 0; -(beta-1)/vol
with the 0.05 vol floor produced a huge score. The cross-sectional normaliser then
scaled every live name down against phantom ones. The sleeve was mostly long/short
funds that did not exist.

**Fix.** live_mask() marks a name live from its first valid price and is applied
before cross-sectional scoring, weight normalisation, and carry ranking. Signal
lookbacks remain NaN until their own history exists. The lead-lag network uses only
live names in each window, and the SVXY sleeve cannot hold before SVXY lists.

**Effect.** BAB Sharpe 0.171 → **0.101**; turnover 3.3× → 3.6×; corr to SPY 0.10 →
**−0.05**. Equal-weight book 0.662 → **0.581**; core (legacy SVXY) 0.718 → **0.608**;
60/40 + 30% core 0.903 → **0.879**; proper-VRP core 0.729 → **0.657**; DSR with all
23 trials 0.589 → **0.496**. The SVXY sleeve itself rises 0.270 → **0.276** because
its pre-2011 zero-weight period is removed.

The direction of the conclusion survives — a 30% core sleeve still improves a 60/40 —
but the standalone core no longer clears SPY on Sharpe (0.61 vs 0.64). That is the
honest reading, and it is why the portfolio overlay, not the sleeve, is the product.

The book combiner holds a not-yet-live sleeve flat (zero return) rather than
renormalising across live sleeves. This is conservative: it does not backfill SVXY
before 2011 or BAB before its style ETFs list, so early book returns are diluted.

**HRP numerical fix.** Masking leaves the SVXY sleeve flat before 2011. HRP's
inverse-variance step divided by zero and emitted warnings; zero-variance sleeves
now receive zero weight and the recursion normalises over live sleeves. HRP
0.378 → **0.344**. The ranking is unchanged.

Reproduce: ./.venv/bin/python3 diagnostics/05_live_mask.py.

## 6. Survivorship (disclosed, not fixed)

The ETF universe is today's liquid list. Funds that closed or never gathered
assets are absent, so the cross-sectional sleeves are **upward-biased**. No free
point-in-time membership exists. The stock momentum sleeve is therefore labelled
and **excluded from the combined book**. This is a caveat, not a correction.

## 7. Multiple testing

Trial registry and Deflated Sharpe Ratio are in `output/metrics.json` and
`diagnostics/trials_diagnostic.json`:

|---|---|

| 2 (final published variants only) | **0.924** |

The 23-trial number is the honest one for the *search process*; the 2-trial
number is the honest one for the *published claim*. Both are reported.

## What not to over-trust

- BAB: 95–97% of its 2008–2010 score was phantom; even masked, 0.10 is noise.
- The stock sleeve: survivorship-biased, and now weak even before that.
- Gated VRP: no 2008 test (VIX3M starts 2009-09), one regime, 5 bps/month is
  optimistic for a variance position.
- Network momentum: one lookback, one `k`, one blend; +0.09 is inside the noise.
- Carry: spot ETFs, not futures; no commodity leg; one 19-year window.
- The DSR: 143 monthly observations is a short sample for a deflation correction.
