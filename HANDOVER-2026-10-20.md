## What changed

### cartograf

1. **Turnover denominator.** `turnover_annual(to, len(R))` used the full panel
   (2003+) although targets start in 2008. Corrected to the traded window.
   Trend turnover 17.4× → **22.1×**; xsmom 8.9× → **11.3×**; value 5.1× → **6.4×**.
2. **Month-end alignment.** The VRP realised-vol series (calendar month-end) was
   exact-timestamp reindexed to the trading-day month-end. Every month whose last
   calendar day was a weekend became NaN and was dropped: VRP sample 200 → **285**
   months. Fixed with `to_period("M")` alignment in `vrp_monthly()`, the core
   book, the SVXY proxy and the monthly benchmark.
   - mean P&L +1.83 → **+2.40** vol points/month; implied > realised 81.5% → **83.5%**.
   - gated VRP Sharpe 0.90 → **1.18**; CAGR 9.3% → **14.1%**.
   - core (trend+BAB+VRP) Sharpe 0.66 → **0.85**.
3. **BAB.** Beta/vol were still computed on zero-filled returns. Now estimated
   from un-filled prices with `min_periods=252/63`, and `live_mask()` requires
   20 observations plus a recent print. BAB Sharpe 0.101 → **−0.083**.
4. **Statistics.** Sortino uses downside deviation; annual Worst/Best exclude the
   partial 2026; DSR is reported on the **overlay** (60/40+30%) as well as the
   sleeve; moving-block bootstrap added.

## Pitfalls (new)

- Monthly series must be aligned by `to_period("M")`, not exact timestamp.
  Trading-day month-ends and calendar month-ends differ whenever the last
  calendar day is a weekend/holiday.
- Turnover denominators must cover the traded window, not the full price panel.
- An overlay that changes exposure must pay turnover; a gross overlay Sharpe is
  not a strategy Sharpe.
- A DSR on the sleeve is not a DSR on the claim. Report both.
- The VRP result is stronger after the alignment fix, but it is still one regime
  (2009-11+), 5 bps/month is optimistic, and the gate failed February 2018.
- BAB is now negative. Do not describe it as a weak survivor; it is a failed
  proxy on this data.
