# AUDIT — inercia (2026-10-20)

_Second hardening pass. The strategy and the book are unchanged; this audit
fixes the statistics, charges the overlays' turnover, and reports the benchmark
and the claim both ways. Reproduce with the commands at the bottom._

## 1. Statistics

- **Sortino** used the standard deviation of negative returns. That is not the
  Sortino denominator; it includes the dispersion of losses around their mean.
  It now uses downside deviation `sqrt(mean(min(r, 0)^2))`. The book's Sortino
  moves 0.73 → **0.80**; the strategy 0.69 → **0.75**.
- **WorstYear / BestYear** now exclude the partial 2026. A nine-month stub is
  not a calendar-year result.
- `CAGR_excess` is still the CAGR of the excess-return series, not CAGR minus
  the risk-free rate. The key is labeled `CAGR_excess` for continuity; the
  monthly table uses `CAGR_excess_series`.

## 2. The overlays now pay for their turnover

`drawdown_control` changes the exposure multiplier; the regime and tilt
overlays change it too. The old code multiplied returns by the exposure and
charged nothing. The new code charges **5 bps per unit of exposure change** and
reports gross and net side by side.

| Overlay | Sharpe gross | Sharpe net | MaxDD net |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 10%-target book (base) | — | 0.546 | −36.3% |
| + drawdown control | 0.565 | **0.543** | −18.7% |
| + regime scaling | 0.627 | 0.627 | −34.1% |
| + both (hardened) | 0.684 | **0.646** | −18.5% |

The drawdown-control overlay's advantage over the base largely disappears once
its own trading costs are charged (0.565 gross → 0.543 net). The combined
hardened book still improves on the base, but the improvement is smaller than
the old 0.630 suggested. The tilt still fails: gross 0.454 / 0.411 / 0.334 at
k = 0.3 / 0.6 / 1.0, against 0.546 for the base.

## 3. Benchmark convention

The body uses a **daily-rebalanced** 60/40 with no costs. A **monthly-rebalanced**
60/40 (SPY/IEF, no rebalancing costs) scores Sharpe **0.911** over 2004–2026,
versus **0.796** daily. The overlay improves the monthly benchmark too:

| Portfolio | CAGR | Vol | Sharpe | MaxDD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 60/40 daily | 8.29% | 10.73% | 0.796 | −31.4% |
| 60/40 monthly | 8.01% | 8.92% | 0.911 | −29.5% |
| 60/40 + 30% book (daily) | 7.32% | 8.27% | 0.895 | −23.1% |
| **60/40 + 30% book (monthly)** | **7.08%** | **6.85%** | **1.036** | **−20.6%** |

The conclusion survives — the overlay raises risk-adjusted return and cuts the
drawdown — but the size depends on the convention. Both are in
`finalbook_metrics.json`.

## 4. DSR on the claim, and a bootstrap

The DSR used to be computed only on the 74/26 book. The product is the 60/40 +
30% overlay, so the claim gets its own deflation:

| Object | Sharpe | DSR (26 trials) | 95% block bootstrap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 74/26 book | 0.581 | 0.763 | — |
| **60/40 + 30% overlay** | **0.895** | **0.987** | **0.499 – 1.289** |

The bootstrap is a moving-block bootstrap: 2,000 draws, 21-day blocks. The
interval is wide; a single path is not evidence. The overlay's lower bound is
still positive, but the width is the honest message.

## 5. Cost stress (net of 5/10/25/50 bps per unit turnover)

| Cost | Trend Sharpe | Carry Sharpe | Book Sharpe | Overlay Sharpe |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 bps | 0.547 | 0.185 | 0.581 | 0.895 |
| 10 bps | 0.457 | 0.146 | 0.484 | 0.866 |
| 25 bps | 0.187 | 0.028 | 0.194 | 0.780 |
| 50 bps | −0.259 | −0.168 | −0.284 | 0.635 |

At 18.9× annual turnover, the trend edge is gone by 25 bps. The overlay still
beats the daily 60/40 at 10 bps (0.866 vs 0.796), is roughly flat at 25 bps
(0.780 vs 0.796), and loses at 50 bps. That is the realistic cost range for a
retail implementation; the 5 bps case is the optimistic one.

## 8. What not to over-trust

- The overlay's bootstrap interval is wide (0.50–1.29).
- The 60/40 benchmark is SPY/IEF, not AGG.
- The 74/26 weights and the 30% overlay are in-sample choices; they are counted
  as trials, but a locked holdout would be stronger.
- The stock sleeve is still survivorship-biased and excluded.
- Costs above 25 bps remove the trend edge; the overlay is a risk reducer, not a
  money printer.
